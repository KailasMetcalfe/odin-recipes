Burger

Desciption

A deliciously juicy cheeseburger.

Ingredients

  • Beef Mince
  • Egg
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce
  • American Cheese
  • Ketchup, Mayo
  • Gherkins
  • Bacon
  • Burger Buns

Steps

  1. Place your beef mince into a bowl and crack an egg into it
  2. With your hand, work the meat until it is incorporated
  3. Then form a patty at your desired size
  4. Now prepare all your toppings. This includes slicing a tomato, shredding some lettuce and chopping a gherkin
  5. Next we will make the sauce. Mix 1 part ketchup to 2 parts mayonaise and pour in a little gherkin brine
  6. Then we come to cooking the meat. Heat up a pan to a high temperature and pour some oil into it.
  7. Once it has come up to temperature place your formed patty into the pan and bacon if desired
  8. After a couple of minutes, once the patty starts to go dark brown, flip it. Do the same for the bacon
  9. If you are uncomfortable with ensuring the meat is fully cooked place in the oven for 5mins once both sides are browned
  10. Once cooked, leave to rest on the side
  11. Then toast your buns in the same pan as where the meat was for a minute or so until crisp
  12. Finally we construct in the following order: Bun, sauce, patty, cheese, bacon, letuce, tomato, gherkin, closing bun
